The Opportunity — Who This Is For

Built for the businesses that run South Africa.

Salons, trades, gyms, events, property, restaurants, practices — the businesses where the owner is also the receptionist, the quoter, and the follow-up department. That's exactly where automation pays.

Salons & BeautyPlumbing & TradesFitness StudiosEvents & HireProperty & RentalsRestaurantsMedical & DentalLegal & Accounting

The opportunity

Most small businesses don't lose clients on price — they lose them on response time. An enquiry that lands at 21:40 and gets answered at 09:15 the next morning has usually already booked with someone else. Studies consistently show the first business to respond wins the majority of the work.

Meanwhile the owner's week disappears into admin: chasing quotes, confirming bookings, reminding no-shows, asking for reviews, following up on cold leads. None of that needs a human. All of it needs to happen — reliably, instantly, every single time. That gap between "should happen" and "actually happens" is the opportunity, and it's enormous in the SME market.

How it actually works

Every pipeline we build follows one honest pattern: trigger → logic → action. A trigger is something that happens in your business — a form is filled in, a booking is requested, a job is marked complete. The logic layer applies your rules: score the lead, check the calendar, wait two days, route to the right person. Then the action fires: a reply is sent, an invoice is generated, a reminder goes out.

We connect to what you already use — your website forms, your calendar, your inbox — so there's nothing new to learn and nothing sensitive leaves your control. Anything with judgement in it (approving a quote, handling a complaint) is routed to a human: you stay the brain, the pipeline is just the hands. And we deliberately don't market the tools underneath: the logic is designed for your business, and the plumbing is swappable — you are never locked into any single app.

Frequently asked questions

Before you reach out,
you probably want to know...

How much does a project actually cost?+

It depends on scope, but most landing pages start around R 3,000–R 5,000, full multi-page sites run R 8,000–R 20,000, and automation add-ons are quoted separately based on what you need. You'll get a fixed, written price before any work begins — never an open-ended hourly rate.

How long does a build take?+

A landing page typically ships in 3–5 days. Multi-page sites take 1–2 weeks. Automation builds run 1–2 weeks depending on complexity. Full packages combining several of these usually land in 2–4 weeks. Rush delivery is available for an extra fee if you're on a deadline.

What do I need to pay upfront?+

A 50% deposit to begin work, and the remaining 50% on delivery. We accept EFT (bank transfer) and PayFast. Nothing is charged before you've approved a written proposal and scope.

What happens after my site goes live?+

You own the site outright. If you'd like us to keep handling updates, backups, and edits, monthly maintenance plans start at R 200/month — but they're optional, not a requirement to launch.

I don't have a logo, copy, or photos yet — can you still help?+

Yes. Most clients start with just an idea. We'll guide you through what's needed during the discovery call and can help source or draft placeholder branding and copy so the build isn't held up.

Can automation really replace manual admin work?+

For repetitive tasks — booking confirmations, lead follow-ups, quote generation, review requests — yes. We wire these into your existing site or WhatsApp/email so they run in the background, 24/7, without you touching a spreadsheet.

How many revisions do I get?+

Every project includes a revision round before sign-off, so you can request changes to layout, copy, and design before final delivery. Larger scope changes outside the original brief are quoted separately and agreed in writing first.
